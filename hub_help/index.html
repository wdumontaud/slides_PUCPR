TOPS

Models of building walls, alone or coupled with the building simulated by Domus. The hub opens on its home: choose a model there; the house logo at the top left brings you back to it.

Everywhere in the hub

House logoHome: models, settings, keyboard shortcuts, help (this page), exit.
? (top right)Help of the model shown, inside the hub. At the top of every help page: this page (⌂ Help home), the help of each model, and ✕ Back to the hub (or Esc).
⏻ (top right, or Exit on the home)Closes the app and stops the server. A running optimisation goes on.
Bell (top right)The messages of this session (the bubbles at the bottom right) with their hour; a dot: an error or a warning you have not seen. An error bubble stays until you click it.
TerminalOne per run: you find it as you left it when you come back to a model.
Top barIcons: settings (gear, Ctrl+,), the parameters (shown / hidden), Simulation | Optimisation, then Geometry, Terminal, Graphs (Ctrl+1 2 3). Hover an icon for its name. The running job and its progress are in the title of the window.
ParametersKept per model. modified: value changed since you opened the model.
