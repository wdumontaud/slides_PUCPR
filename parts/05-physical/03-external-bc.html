Convection: wind increasing with height\cite{mcadams1954}

\[ h_{\infty,1}(y,t) = h_{10} + h_{11}\,\frac{v_\infty(t)}{v_0}\,\Big(\frac{y}{y_0}\Big)^{\beta} \]
\(v_\infty\): wind speed of the weather file at the reference height \(y_0\); \(\beta\): exponent of the urban wind profile.

Radiation absorbed / emitted by the façade

\[ q_{\infty,1}(\x,t) = \underbrace{q_{sw}(\x,t)}_{\text{short-wave (sun)}} + \underbrace{q_{\ell w}(\x,t)}_{\text{long-wave (surroundings)}} \]
both depend on the position on the façade: sunlit or shaded part, what each point sees.
