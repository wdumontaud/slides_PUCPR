Short-wave: solar radiation

\[ q_{sw}(\x,t) = \alpha\Big(\underbrace{\htmlClass{hl-sun}{q_{dr}(\x,t)}}_{\text{direct}} + \underbrace{q_{df}(\x,t)}_{\text{diffuse}} + \underbrace{q_{rf}(\x,t)}_{\text{reflected}}\Big) \]
\(q_{dr}=0\) on the shaded part of the façade: the sunlit / shadow edge moves during the day.

Long-wave: exchanges with the surroundings

\[ \begin{aligned} q_{\ell w}(\x,t) = \ & \varepsilon\sigma T^4(\x,t) \\ & - \varepsilon\sigma\Big( \underbrace{F_{1\text{-}g}(\x)\,T_g^4(t)}_{\text{ground}} + \underbrace{F_{1\text{-}f}(\x)\,T_f^4(t)}_{\text{front building}} \\ & + \underbrace{F_{1\text{-}s}(\x)\,T_s^4(t)}_{\text{sky}} + \underbrace{\int_{\Gamma_1} F_{1\text{-}1}(\x,\x')\,T^4(\x',t)\,\dd\Gamma_1(\x')}_{\text{wall itself}}\Big) \end{aligned} \]
view factors \(F\) depend on \(\x\): a curved façade also sees itself (its hollows).
