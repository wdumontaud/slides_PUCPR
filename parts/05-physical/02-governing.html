2D transient heat diffusion

\[ c(\x)\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\Big(\lambda(\x)\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial \,x}\Big) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\Big(\lambda(\,\x)\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial \,y}\Big), \qquad \x \in \Omega,\ t \in\, ]\,0,t_f\,] \]
\(\Omega=\Omega_1\cup\Omega_2\): the properties \(\lambda,\,c\) depend on the material, hence on the position.

Initial state

\[ T(\x,0) = T_0(\x), \qquad \x \in \Omega \]
\(T_0\) consistent with the boundary conditions.

Robin conditions, varying in space and time

\[ -\lambda\,\nabla T\cdot\n = h_{\infty,j}(y,t)\,\big(T_{\infty,j}(t)-T(\x,t)\big) + q_{\infty,j}(\x,t) \]
on each boundary \(\x\in\Gamma_j,\ j\in\{1,2,3,4\}\): convection with \(h_{\infty,j}\) and radiation \(q_{\infty,j}\).

Interface between the two materials

\[ T\big|_{\x^+} = T\big|_{\x^-}, \qquad \lambda\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial n^+}\Big|_{\x^+} = \lambda\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial n^-}\Big|_{\x^-}, \qquad \x\in\Gamma_i \]
continuity of temperature and heat flux: the interface \(\Gamma_i\) is the design variable.
