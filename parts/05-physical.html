urban scene simulated with Domus
⟶ one façade unit of the street
  • studied wall of height \(H\) and thickness \(L\)
  • front building of height \(\Delta Y\) at \(\Delta X\)
  • boundaries \(\Gamma_1\) (outside), \(\Gamma_3\) (inside), \(\Gamma_2\), \(\Gamma_4\) (bottom, top)

2D transient heat diffusion

\[ c(\x)\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\Big(\lambda(\x)\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial \,x}\Big) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\Big(\lambda(\,\x)\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial \,y}\Big), \qquad \x \in \Omega,\ t \in\, ]\,0,t_f\,] \]
\(\Omega=\Omega_1\cup\Omega_2\): the properties \(\lambda,\,c\) depend on the material, hence on the position.

Initial state

\[ T(\x,0) = T_0(\x), \qquad \x \in \Omega \]
\(T_0\) consistent with the boundary conditions.

Robin conditions, varying in space and time

\[ -\lambda\,\nabla T\cdot\n = h_{\infty,j}(y,t)\,\big(T_{\infty,j}(t)-T(\x,t)\big) + q_{\infty,j}(\x,t) \]
on each boundary \(\x\in\Gamma_j,\ j\in\{1,2,3,4\}\): convection with \(h_{\infty,j}\) and radiation \(q_{\infty,j}\).

Interface between the two materials

\[ T\big|_{\x^+} = T\big|_{\x^-}, \qquad \lambda\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial n^+}\Big|_{\x^+} = \lambda\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial n^-}\Big|_{\x^-}, \qquad \x\in\Gamma_i \]
continuity of temperature and heat flux: the interface \(\Gamma_i\) is the design variable.

Convection: wind increasing with height\cite{mcadams1954}

\[ h_{\infty,1}(y,t) = h_{10} + h_{11}\,\frac{v_\infty(t)}{v_0}\,\Big(\frac{y}{y_0}\Big)^{\beta} \]
\(v_\infty\): wind speed of the weather file at the reference height \(y_0\); \(\beta\): exponent of the urban wind profile.

Radiation absorbed / emitted by the façade

\[ q_{\infty,1}(\x,t) = \underbrace{q_{sw}(\x,t)}_{\text{short-wave (sun)}} + \underbrace{q_{\ell w}(\x,t)}_{\text{long-wave (surroundings)}} \]
both depend on the position on the façade: sunlit or shaded part, what each point sees.

Short-wave: solar radiation

\[ q_{sw}(\x,t) = \alpha\Big(\underbrace{\htmlClass{hl-sun}{q_{dr}(\x,t)}}_{\text{direct}} + \underbrace{q_{df}(\x,t)}_{\text{diffuse}} + \underbrace{q_{rf}(\x,t)}_{\text{reflected}}\Big) \]
\(q_{dr}=0\) on the shaded part of the façade: the sunlit / shadow edge moves during the day.

Long-wave: exchanges with the surroundings

\[ \begin{aligned} q_{\ell w}(\x,t) = \ & \varepsilon\sigma T^4(\x,t) \\ & - \varepsilon\sigma\Big( \underbrace{F_{1\text{-}g}(\x)\,T_g^4(t)}_{\text{ground}} + \underbrace{F_{1\text{-}f}(\x)\,T_f^4(t)}_{\text{front building}} \\ & + \underbrace{F_{1\text{-}s}(\x)\,T_s^4(t)}_{\text{sky}} + \underbrace{\int_{\Gamma_1} F_{1\text{-}1}(\x,\x')\,T^4(\x',t)\,\dd\Gamma_1(\x')}_{\text{wall itself}}\Big) \end{aligned} \]
view factors \(F\) depend on \(\x\): a curved façade also sees itself (its hollows).

Convection varying with height: corner model\cite{santos2009}

\[ h_{\infty,3}(y) = h_{30} + \big(h_{31}-h_{30}\big)\,\sin\!\Big(\frac{\pi\,y}{H}\Big) \]
lower exchange near the floor and ceiling corners.

Long-wave radiation with the room

\[ \begin{gathered} q_{\infty,3}(\x,t) = \varepsilon_3\,\sigma\,\big(T^4(\x,t) - T_r^4(t)\big), \\ T_r(t) = \tfrac12\big(T_{\infty,3}(t) + \langle T(\x,t)\rangle_{\Gamma_3}\big) \end{gathered} \]
mean radiant temperature \(T_r\) \(\approx\) mean of the air and of the inside face. In the co-simulation, \(T_{\infty,3}\) comes from the Domus zone.

Top and bottom: adiabatic

\[ -\lambda\,\nabla T\cdot\n = 0, \qquad \x\in\Gamma_j,\ j\in\{2,4\} \]
