Block scale: the layout of the holes inside a brick, a block or a printed infill is optimised, alone, as a unit. The wall and its boundary conditions are not part of the problem.
Building scale: form, orientation, envelope and systems are optimised together. The wall is one design parameter among many, not a field to be optimised.
