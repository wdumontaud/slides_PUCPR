1. Domus computes at \(t_n\) the surface temperatures, the weather and the solar data of each wall surface, and the zone air.
  2. Each 2D model computes \(q_{sw}\), \(q_{\ell w}\) and advances from \(t_n\) to \(t_{n+1}=t_n+\Delta t_c\), with its own smaller steps \(\delta t\).
  3. It returns the mean temperatures of \(\Gamma_1\) and \(\Gamma_3\), and the mean convective coefficient inside, at \(t_{n+1}\).
  4. Domus updates the street and the room with them, then the next step starts.
⟶ \(\Delta t_c\) sets the accuracy of the coupling: the exchange is made once per step.
